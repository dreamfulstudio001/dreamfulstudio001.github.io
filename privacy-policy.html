01About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Tile Midnight Secrets (the “Application”), a mobile game provided by Dreamful Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The Application is free to download and includes advertisements and optional in-app purchases.

This policy explains how information connected with your use of the Application and your communications with us may be processed. The Application is intended only for people aged 18 and over.

Device and technical information
Device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, app-instance identifiers, and advertising identifiers where available. Advertising and measurement services may infer an approximate location from an IP address.
App usage and advertising activity
App sessions, gameplay interactions, time spent in the Application, and interactions with advertisements, such as views and clicks.
Crash and diagnostic information
Crash reports, error logs, device information, and technical events used to understand failures and improve stability.
Purchase-related information
Product identifiers, transaction references, purchase status, and information needed to provide or restore purchased content. Payments are handled through the relevant app store; its privacy policy explains how it processes payment information.
Information you send to us
Your email address, the contents of support or privacy requests, and any information you choose to include when contacting us.

03How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and support the Application.

  • Deliver advertisements and measure advertising and campaign performance.

  • Respond to support requests and privacy inquiries.
  • Detect fraud, protect the Application, and meet legal obligations.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as reliability and fraud prevention, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent where required. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

06Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, handling requests, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information and the reason it is processed; we do not specify one fixed period for all data.

When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable retention requirements. Third-party providers may apply their own retention rules as described in their policies.

To request deletion of information associated with you, email dreamfulstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Tile Midnight Secrets — Data deletion request.” Describe your request and include relevant app or transaction identifiers only if needed to help locate the information. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

We may request information reasonably needed to verify and fulfill your request. Certain information may need to be retained when required or permitted by law. If your request concerns information independently controlled by a third-party provider, its own privacy procedures may also apply.
